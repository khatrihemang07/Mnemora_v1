AI Module: Conversation page

Stage 1 of the AI prototype, rebuilt from Mnemora's real shell and Components (sizes and tokens copied from Sidebar, TreeRow, TitleBar, PageHeader, ComposerDock, Sheet, Popover) and from research into Claude, ChatGPT, Perplexity, NotebookLM, Zed, Pi and Raycast. Click an option card to preview it live in the mock above (cards tagged wireframe are sketches only). Nothing is picked here; tell me your picks in the session.

How to use: choose a state with the buttons, then click option cards. Click the Model chip, the conversation list icon and the Source chips in the mock: they open the real pickers (Popover on desktop, Sheet on phone). Data is made up. Use Dark to check dark.
Fixed already: Module is default-off; one Run per Conversation; Providers (Anthropic, OpenRouter, OpenAI-compatible, Local AI Wrapper); the Wrapper shows the answer whole, native Providers stream it; the composer is never focused when the page opens (PR #82); no new raw colours (the only new token value is --hue-ai in dark, which today has none).
