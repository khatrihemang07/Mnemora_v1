Journal: prototypes for every decision

Two parts. Part 1 is a working mock of the Journal on desktop and phone, built from Mnemora's real tokens. Switch between the three overall concepts and try it: write, edit, delete and undo, move an Entry to another Page, use the calendar and search. Part 2 is the catalogue: every low-level decision, each with options, a recommendation and a small live demo. Click an option to pick it, add a note if you like, then press Copy my picks at the bottom and paste the result into the chat. Options tagged live also change the mock in Part 1.

IDDecision
D1Entry is the unit. Each Entry has its own unique id. It has no title, and a Page can hold any number of Entries.
D4 / D9Page means all Entries for one date, like a paper diary page. New Entries go on today's Page by default; you can add to or edit any past Page. In v1 a Page is only a container, but more per-Page data is expected later.
D5Every Entry keeps its written timestamp (date + time, in the time zone it was written in) and shows it with a faint "edited" mark. The date part is left out when it matches the Page's date: 08:12, but Mon 19 Oct, 09:12 for an Entry written later (where it sits: N5).
D6Entries on a Page are ordered oldest first. Pages are ordered newest first.
D7A blank Entry is never saved. Clearing an existing Entry's text does not delete it: finishing shows "Empty entries aren't saved. Kept the previous text." and the old text stays. A composer Entry that is cleared and finished (any path) is discarded with an Undo toast, "Entry discarded"; while focus stays in the box, empty text just waits.
D8No late-night handling: a new Entry always defaults to today's Page.
D10An Entry can move to another Page by changing its Page date. Its written timestamp never changes.
D11Browsing is one continuous scroll of Pages. Dates with no Entries are skipped silently, and the calendar is how you reach them.
D2 / D3Bodies are Markdown with live styling. v1 also includes full-text search and calendar navigation. Mood, tags and photos are left out of v1.
